# Attention & Exception Design

Define attention source, severity, visibility, action, destination workspace/filter, and resolution state. Prefer actionable queues such as pending KYC, blocked handovers, returning vehicles, unpaid bookings, or maintenance over decorative alerts. Never fabricate attention data.
