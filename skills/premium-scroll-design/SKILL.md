# Premium Scroll Design

1. Read `docs/PREMIUM_WEBSITE_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
2. Identify the narrative purpose of each major section.
3. Define the visual anchor and hierarchy.
4. Choose composition before components.
5. Add motion only where it communicates progression, state, or depth.
6. Build reduced-motion equivalents.
7. Verify mobile separately from desktop.
8. Verify scroll performance and avoid layout-triggering animation.
9. Capture screenshots at key scroll positions.
10. Record evidence in the relevant design state/QA documents.
