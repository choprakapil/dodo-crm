# Entity Relationship Intelligence

Map the real domain graph from schema/API/docs.

For each relationship define:
- direction/cardinality when known;
- useful summary fields;
- contextual drill-down;
- navigation target;
- audit/history relationship.

Prefer contextual links/drawers over duplicating entire related records.
