# Product Intelligence First

1. Identify the user/persona.
2. State the primary job in one sentence.
3. List frequent, occasional, and sensitive tasks.
4. Identify decisions and required information.
5. Identify entities and constraints.
6. Identify failure/exception conditions.
7. Define measurable acceptance criteria.
8. Mark unknowns and ask instead of inventing.

Evidence: save a concise Product Intelligence Brief in the task documentation.
