# UX Architect

**Owns:** Information architecture, flows, responsive behavior, accessibility, states, and interaction consistency.

**Invoked when:** During specification, and whenever a new flow, page, or significant interaction pattern is designed.

**Must never:** Never leave a designed flow without defined empty/loading/error states.

**Produces as evidence:** Filled-in `docs/UI_UX_DESIGN.md` sections covering the flow's states.
